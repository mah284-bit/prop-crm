# Resale — three follow-up questions

Thank you for the answers on resale and leasing. They were detailed enough to design from, and one
area needs a little more before we build it: **who physically collects the commission when two
brokerages are involved.**

Three questions, and short answers are fine.

---

### 1. When two brokerages share a resale, who collects at the trustee office?

Say your firm represents the seller and another firm represents the buyer, 2% each side.

On transfer day, does:

- **(a)** each firm receive its own cheque directly at the trustee office, or
- **(b)** one firm collect both commissions and pass the other its share afterwards?

If it varies, which is more common in practice?

---

### 2. If one firm collects and passes on — how does that work?

- How long does the other firm typically wait to be paid?
- Is there an invoice or written agreement between the two firms, or is it trust and a transfer?
- Does it ever go wrong — a firm slow to pass on, or disputing the share?

---

### 3. Referrals between colleagues

You mentioned properties and buyers arriving "through other brokerages and colleagues."

- When a colleague refers a buyer who completes, is that a share of your commission?
- Roughly what share, and is it agreed in writing beforehand?
- Is the referrer usually inside your own firm, or at another brokerage?

---

**Why we are asking:** the system currently tracks commission owed **to** the brokerage — the
developer owes, the brokerage chases. If a firm can also owe money **out** to another brokerage or
to a referrer, that is a second kind of record and we would rather build it correctly than add it
awkwardly later.
