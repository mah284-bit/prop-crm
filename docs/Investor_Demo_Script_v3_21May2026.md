**What you say:**

> "Above the table — the reference line — shows what's on the table right now. The latest proposal terms in one line.
>

>

**Then navigate to 📊 Upfront tab to show the architectural separation:**

**What's on screen (Upfront tab):**
- Left: 💸 Buyer Outflow card (Net Price + DLD + Oqood + Annual Maintenance)
- Right: 💼 Broker Commission card (separate, AED 94,831 at 4%)

**What you say:**

> "And here's an architectural choice that matters.
>

>
> On the RIGHT — what the broker earns. Completely separate. Paid by developer, not by buyer. We never mix these.
>
> This isn't a UI choice. It's a compliance choice. UAE brokers get audited. Their license depends on clean financial separation. We made the architecture enforce it."

**Investor takeaway:** ★★★★★
- Negotiation lifecycle visible
- Reference line solves "what's on the table"

## INVESTOR Q&A — TOP QUESTIONS + ANSWERS

### Q: "How is PropPulse data acquired?"
A: "AI agents scraping public sources — RERA, DLD project registry, developer websites, news announcements. Verification queue with confidence scoring. We can show you the source attribution per data point. As we add brokerages, our 'In My Inventory' signal becomes a network effect — what brokers actually transact on is data nobody else has."

### Q: "What's stopping competitors from copying this?"
A: "Three barriers. One — the architecture (compliance + intelligence + workflow integrated, not bolted on). Two — the data (90 projects, 20 developers, growing every week with AI agents). Three — the broker network (every brokerage we onboard contributes verification signal). By month 18, our intelligence layer is uncatchable."

### Q: "Why UAE specifically?"
A: "Three reasons. One — UAE has unique compliance (DLD fees, master agreements, off-plan vs ready) that generic CRMs don't model. Two — brokers earn 2-4% on AED 2-10M deals — high enough to pay for software protecting their license. Three — successful UAE = springboard to Saudi (Vision 2030 buildout) and broader GCC."

### Q: "Tech stack?"
A: "React + Supabase (Postgres). Modern, type-safe, deployable in hours. Supabase gives us Postgres + RLS for multi-tenant isolation. We move fast. Phase 2 we may add specialized services for PDF, OCR, etc."

### Q: "Multi-tenant security?"
A: "Row-Level Security on every table. Each brokerage's data isolated by company_id, enforced server-side, not client-side. We can run penetration tests with your engineering team before pilot expansion."

### Q: "Revenue model?"
A: "SaaS per-agent per-month. Tiered by features (Basic, Pro with AI Coach, Enterprise with custom integrations). ~AED 200-400 per agent. Plus PropPulse intelligence layer monetizable separately as data subscription. Plus future marketplace commissions on developer-side integrations."

### Q: "Path to profitability?"
A: "First 10 brokerages = product-market fit. 50 brokerages = positive unit economics. 200+ brokerages = strong margin. UAE market alone is 800+ active brokerages — significant headroom."

### Q: "What's broken or pre-MVP?"
A: "Honest answer — three things being polished. One: brochure PDFs for all 90 projects (sourcing problem, not engineering). Two: AI brochure scanner (built, needs re-enablement). Three: PDF generation for broker proposals (Phase 2 work, designed, scheduled for July). None of these is structural."
