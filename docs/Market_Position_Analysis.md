## AI landscape 2026
82% of agents use AI (writing 78%, chatbots 47%, market analysis 39%). Table stakes: behavioral lead
scoring, AI follow-up drafting, 24/7 qualifying assistants. Frontier: seller-intent prediction,
'who to call today' intelligence, agentic workflow execution. Industry direction = 'system of
intelligence, not records' - our evidence-based auto-advance (Day 64 capture) matches the frontier.
Our real AI (PDF-extract promote, Coach, dedup summary) beats bolted-on chat; gaps: lead scoring,
AI follow-up drafting.

## Verdict
NOT over-engineered where it counts - deal-spine depth = the moat. Slightly ahead of need: groups/
branch plumbing, org chart. UNDER vs table stakes: portal ingestion (THE gap), RERA forms, WhatsApp.
Post-tester roadmap: (1) portal feed connectors - ANY source as a channel (portals, WhatsApp, website,
referral) with same dedup/routing = stronger than 2-portal silo; (2) RERA forms; (3) make existing AI
visible (saturation narration, next-best-action) before chatbots; (4) rename.
