
## Phase 2.3B Multi-PDF Bundle & Selective Send (Founder Vision, Day 32)

**Vision:** Property Pack generates 3 integrated PDFs (Project, Unit, Proposal). Broker selects which to send, chooses channel (Email/WhatsApp/Download), sends bundle.

**Current:** Single Property Pack PDF + Share individual channels

**Phase 2.3B v2 (post-MVP feedback):**

- ProposalPDF (from opp.proposal_versions[0] — current accepted V_latest)

**Design principle:** Broker sees what they want to send, sends it once. No repetition.

**Effort:** 3-4 hours (ProposalPDF component + bundling logic)
